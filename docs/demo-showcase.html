Demo Steps

STEP 1

Connect & Fund

  • Open the dashboard
  • Connect a Sui testnet wallet (Sui Wallet, Suiet, etc.)
  • Click + FAUCET to mint 1000 VEIL test tokens
What to look for:
  • Wallet connects and address appears in sidebar
  • Public balance updates to 1000.000000 VEIL
  • Transaction link appears in notification
STEP 2

Anonymous Deposit

  • In the Deposit tab, select a denomination (100, 500, or 1000 VEIL)
  • Click Deposit & Register
  • A Poseidon commitment is computed client-side and submitted via PTB
What to look for:
  • Standard denominations only (100/500/1000) -- resists correlation analysis
  • "Shielded Balance" card updates with deposited amount
  • Commitment hash visible in transaction events on Suiscan
  • No amount or sender identity in emitted events
STEP 3

Anonymous Transfer (Below Threshold)

  • Switch to the Transfer tab
  • Enter an amount (e.g., 50 VEIL)
  • Click Shielded Transfer
  • A Groth16 proof is generated in-browser (~2s)
What to look for:
  • 5-step progress indicator: commitment, proof, nullifier, submit, confirm
  • Threshold progress bar shows cumulative spending vs. 1000 VEIL limit
  • Old UTXO commitment consumed, new one created (event on-chain)
  • Proof generation time displayed
STEP 4

Hit the Threshold

  • Make multiple transfers until cumulative spending approaches 1000 VEIL
  • At 70%: yellow warning -- "KYC may be required soon"
  • At 100%: transfer blocked -- "Threshold exceeded"
What to look for:
  • Progress bar color shifts: green → yellow (70%) → red (100%)
  • Warning message appears before reaching the limit
  • Transfer button disabled when threshold exceeded
  • This is the core privacy/compliance tradeoff in action
STEP 5

Compliant Transfer (Above Threshold)

  • In the sidebar, open Credential Manager
  • Click [Demo] Generate Test Credential
  • Switch to the Compliant Transfer tab
  • Select credential, enter amount, submit
What to look for:
  • Two Groth16 proofs generated (transfer + compliance) -- both verified atomically
  • Amount encrypted for auditor via ECDH P-256 + AES-GCM
  • Credential nullifier is context-bound (unique per transfer, not reusable)
  • No identity linked across transfers -- only the auditor can decrypt amounts
STEP 6

Verify On-Chain

  • All transfers emit privacy-preserving events (no amounts, no addresses)
  • ComplianceVerifiedEvent contains the auditor-encrypted amount
  • Pool balance is verifiable on Suiscan
What to look for:
  • Event logs contain nullifiers and commitments but no plaintext data
  • Pool total_deposits matches sum of all deposits
  • Nullifier set grows with each transfer (prevents double-spend)
  • Compliance events are only readable by the designated auditor key

Key Technical Details

Privacy Guarantees

  • UTXO model: old note consumed, new note created per transfer
  • Identity-bound commitments: Poseidon(value, rand, userSecret)
  • Note-based nullifiers: multiple transfers per epoch
  • Standard deposit denominations: resist amount correlation
  • No sender/recipient/amount in any event

Compliance Architecture

  • Cumulative spending limit (1000 VEIL per epoch)
  • Anonymous below threshold, KYC required above
  • Dual Groth16 verification (transfer + compliance)
  • Auditor-encrypted amounts (ECDH + AES-GCM)
  • Context-bound credential nullifiers (one-use per transfer)
